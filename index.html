Privacy Policy

CoinSteady

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This policy explains how the CoinSteady app treats information. CoinSteady is a market scanner. It shows publicly available cryptocurrency prices and charts. It is not a wallet, exchange, or brokerage, and it does not hold funds.

Who we are

CoinSteady is published by Volodymyr Budko. Contact: eyeappies@protonmail.com

Purchases

CoinSteady may offer a free trial, then a paid subscription. Payment is processed by Google Play (or Apple, if you subscribe there), not by CoinSteady. We do not see or store your card number, bank details, or billing address.

The store bills the subscription, including after a trial ends, under its own terms and privacy policy. It gives us a purchase record, such as an order id and whether the trial or subscription is active. We use that only to unlock the app. We do not sell that record. Cancel or manage the subscription in the store account settings.

Information the app collects

CoinSteady does not ask you to create an account. We do not ask for your name, email, or phone number inside the app, and we do not collect payment details. Purchases are handled by the store, as described above.

The app requests public market data (prices, charts, listings) from third-party market sources so it can display coins. Those requests do not include your identity.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use the app to build an advertising profile.

Information your device or stores may process

Google Play, Apple, and your device may process standard technical data when you download or open an app, such as app version, device type, and crash logs, under their own policies. If we later add analytics or crash reporting, this page will name the provider and what it receives before that build ships.

Third parties

Chart and listing data comes from public market sources, which may include CoinMarketCap and exchanges such as Binance, Bybit, KuCoin, Gate, and MEXC. Those services receive the network request needed to return public prices. They do not receive an account from us, because the app has none.

We do not share personal information with data brokers.

Children

CoinSteady is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Retention and deletion

Because the app does not create an account or store a profile on our servers, there is no user profile to delete. Uninstalling the app removes it from your device. To ask a question about this policy, email eyeappies@protonmail.com.

Changes

If the app starts collecting account data, analytics, or payments, this page will be updated before that version is released. The date at the top will change.
